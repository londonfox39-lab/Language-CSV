4.0 Strategy and Implementation Summary

Complete section text from the business plan.

The main goal of MH is to provide active seniors with resources access to quality care, living assistance, and above standard housing. The organization will look to partner with veterans, senior associations, medical groups, churches, and city programs to establish a recognizable and trusted name in the community. The MH executive team has dedicated their lives and careers assisting others and will use that dedication and commitment as they create a residence that makes a difference, and is filled with happy, healthy, and dignified aging adults. The company will operate with the goal to provide an environment that not only provides essential care, but also fosters a sense of community, familiarity, and well-being for families and their loved ones.

4.1 Competitive Edge

The new boutique assisted living center will earn a competitive advantage by providing quality staffing, meaningful programming, and unique service offerings all at an affordable cost. This model is attractive to the elders and to their adult children whose focus is their parents’ safety and wellbeing. MH will integrate an approach to care that encompasses physical, mental, emotional, and social well-being. Unlike facilities that primarily focus on physical health and daily living activities, the MH facility will incorporate programs that address all aspects of the resident’s wellness.

There are 536 assisted living communities in the Detroit Metro Area. Madison House will stand out from those and from these three prototype competitors: Magenta Care Group, Green House Project, and St. Joseph Manor.

Unlike facilities like Magenta Care Group, Madison House is a newly renovated home, equipped with modern amenities, technology, and a clean, green environmentally conscious operating philosophy and staff. One of the MH owner/operators is a senior and understands the unique needs of its residents’ needs and desires. MH offers a unique wellness-focused lifestyle and is situated only seven minutes from Henry Ford Hospital and newly renovated areas of downtown Detroit.

Living in MH is as close as an aging adult can get to living independently while enjoying all the perks of personal care and the ability to share communal space and time at leisure. Unlike living in a large facility like St. Joseph, MH focuses on enriching the lives of the individual residents.

Like the Green House Project, MH puts its care philosophy first--the belief that all people deserve the right to live a long, active and healthy life, in an environment that adds dignity, respect, independence and gives the loving human interaction they deserve. The Green House Project got it right that with fewer residents and less stress on staff, everyone lives a more full-filling life and even reverses some of the aging process. MH has taken this philosophy another step further by not taking on more than five residents in this care home.

As a non-profit and through grant funding MH can make sure that the home never has to turn anyone away for lack of money. Aging brings back our humanity, allows us to look back on our lives, to appreciate the past and the people that filled our memories of it. MH will give aging adults the shelter and support to do that.

Magenta Care Group is an assisted living home in the city of Detroit. Its monthly cost is $4,000, which is lower than the $4,750 average monthly cost of care in the metro area. Magenta Care Group prioritizes care and medical services for its residents. It is a single family home nestled a central Detroit neighborhood. This offers its residents a small, close-knit environment with personalized attention. They have a 24-hour staff that assist residents with bathing, dressing, medication management, and coordination with healthcare providers. Magenta Care Groups weakness is that though they are a smaller home, they have their residents packed into a three bedroom house. Residents share dingy outdated rooms in this house built in the 1940's. Though they create a close knit community, the environment is not inspiring, nor environmentally conscious.

St. Joseph Manor is an assisted living facility in the Detroit area that also offers independent living and care for up to one hundred and forty residents. Its estimated costs start at $4,656. It is located near the Detroit River and Lake St. Clair. St. Joseph Manor has a large campus and provides medical care and services tailored to its residents’ individual needs. They also provide 24-hour supervision, assistance with daily activities and medication management. St. Joseph Manor goes to the opposite extreme of Magenta Care Group. With over 140 residents personal care suffers, residents become herded, kept on schedule, and live by rules and regulations. This is an operation, not a way of living. St. Joseph falls into the classic fare of assisted living homes and this is what aging people fear and are trying to get away from. Research has proved that these institutions do not have the best results.

The Green House Project is a not for profit assisted living community with one-hundred locations established in cities across the US. There are seven located in Michigan and two in the Detroit metro area. Green House facilities are designed to a better option than larger facilities. By only accepting ten to twelve residents in their facilities, they can provide better care for their residents but not a better work environment for their staff. Their homes are designed with the kitchen as the heart of their facilities. Their philosophy about humanizing eldercare drives their facilities and demonstrates results. The Green House Project has proven that by putting residents into smaller communities centered around living and their humanness, their residents live a longer more fulfilled life. During COVID residents in Green House Project homes had 30% on average fewer infections than larger homes. The weakness of The Green House Project is that their homes are still built to house a larger number of residents. Though they are guided by the theory of bringing the aging back to a home like environment, their buildings are still institutions and lack the personal soft environment we all consider our homes.

4.2 Marketing Strategy

MH understands the importance of marketing at a high level. Even though the residents are in an aging demographic, many of them are the first generation to interface with computers and the internet. Therefore, the business model is designed to leverage the sixty-five years of combined business experience and relationships to not just build referral and partnership networks, but to also build an online presence and AI supported marketing and database analytics pipeline.

MH will research, reach out to, and build relationships online and offline with the community, social influencers, and organizations both in Michigan and throughout the nation. Online advertising is a powerful tool to build engagement and the company has already garnered experience using YouTube and Facebook to reach specific demographics. These are particularly the platforms that the residents interact on.

In an actual Facebook Boost case study conducted by the MH Operations Manager, with only a $7,500 spend on marketing, he was able to reach 3,639,470 people. Of those 3,639,470, a mind-blowing 1,300,000 viewed the posted content. That is more than 50% of the total potential customer base. If the organization could reach the customer base, plus their family and loved ones, MH could become a Michigan household name.

The organization can both increase outreach, pinpoint the client base, and learn more about their specific likes and needs by implementing AI (artificial intelligence) driven marketing and predictive analytics. Human behavior and taste are constantly changing, and with predictive analytics, MH takes the assumptions out of future projections. The team will leverage web data analytics and AI driven marketing platforms to optimize outreach and heighten the resident experience at MH.

As mentioned above, outside of online marketing, the aging community is built up of doctors, hospitals, and a host of other agencies and foundations that share resources and are always looking for ways to support the community. Becoming a part of this network, in some cases may do as much for MH as online AI marketing. The small, individualized boutique experience is a major advantage and keeps them from operating with an increase the numbers mindset and instead shifts to an increase the satisfaction and standard of living one.

4.3 Sales Strategy

The boutique nature of MH allows it to recruit residents by referral from local doctors, churches, social service offices, aging organizations, and word of mouth. As MH grows and acquires more properties, it will add a sales position and hire an answering service that will forward inquiries to the General Manager and Sales Representative.

The intake process begins with the first connection between the resident and the organization. Potential residents can arrange for a tour or discuss any questions they or their family members may have. Potential residents will go through an evaluation process to make sure they are a good fit for MH and specifically that the center can provide the excellent care that they need. Residents will also complete an assessment and survey to outline their specific needs, which helps the staff create a customized care plan. Once they have decided to reside at MH, a contract outlining the costs and resident responsibilities will be signed by both parties.

The residency agreements are designed to be transparent and easy to understand, clearly outlining the services, fees, and terms of residency. MH offers flexible payment options to accommodate the financial situations of different families. This includes structured payment plans for the registration fee and the possibility of adjusting services as the resident's needs change. The goal is to never turn away a resident because they are unable to pay so the management will work with each resident based on their needs. Some insurance payments such as long-term care insurance and VA benefit insurances will also be accepted. All outreach and inquires will be kept in a secure database.

4.4 Milestones

The following milestones ensure a solid approach to establishing the assisted living facility. They cover the critical areas of property and facility readiness, staffing, marketing, and financial stability during the initial operational phase. Achieving these milestones will set a solid foundation for the facility, enabling it to deliver high-quality care, meet business objectives, and establish itself as a preferred choice for assisted living in the targeted community.

MH research and support network milestones will run through all five stages of the business startup. It is imperative that the organization network with medical professionals and groups to collaborate with the right practitioners and practices for the residents. The owners will attend all the major conferences regarding aging, caring for the aging, advancements in technologies, and operational standards. This process will help them enhance the services and the design of the boutique home. They intend to become a voice in the community and would like to enable residents to continue being active members of the community as well. MH will not just be a place for seniors to rest-away in their twilight, but for them to pick up a mantel as elders of any community should they want to.

The organization will begin to renovate the existing property to create a living situation suitable for four to five aging adults. MH will provide the same convenience, without packing its residents into facilities that are too large to have the same comforts as home. Residents will have privacy when needed and even though they are in a single-family home, the renovations to the home will still give them the freedom and space of living in individual apartments.

MH will install IT, furniture, and other operational stems. The planning phase of this process will equip each resident living quarters with unintrusive state of the art communications with their family, friends, on-site staff, and Madison House executives. This also includes certain AI features for monitoring the residents’ vitals, doctor visits, nutrition, and fitness.

It is of utmost importance that MH is staffed with respectful, patient, loving, and skilled staff. The owners have gone through the process of finding trustworthy health care providers for their own aging parents and understand it is critical for the residents and their family to have peace of mind. They will conduct background checks and personality reviews before starting a trial period evaluation.

Marketing will also be a phase that begins far into the earlier milestones but culminates in this period prior to opening. Using an established network of partners and contracting with a marketing firms MH intends to become a recognizable and sought out presence in the community. Once all milestones are complete, the center will be ready to open the doors to residents. The owners aim to eventually assist all aging persons in need of housing, but the success and sustained harmony of Madison House residents requires a basis for compatibility and an open mind for setting out on such a new adventure together.
