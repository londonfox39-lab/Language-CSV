1.0 Executive Summary

Madison House, located in the Grand River community of Detroit Michigan, will be a boutique assisted living home for active seniors and veterans. The facility will provide twenty-four-hour care, nutrition, fitness plans, transportation and interface with medical teams for the in-house clients aging-in-place. The owners believe that no citizen who has worked their whole life and contributed to our great society should have to live their twilight years wanting or being priced out of a decent place to live.

Madison House will provide a community where seniors and others who need assistance can live, thrive, and reach their full mental, physical, and social potential. This is accomplished in a loving environment where all the resident needs can be met. The company will operate a private assisted living facility in Michigan to provide quality long-term care for the elderly and disabled. The new facility will provide a safe, home-like living environment for residents and allow them to achieve a high quality of living in a comforting and supportive environment.

The company will provide residents with personalized care and programming to promote an increased state of well-being, improve quality of life, and allow for overall physical and mental healthcare. Madison House ownership is committed to delivering outstanding amenities to residents while focusing on providing quality and patient-centered care.

The company was founded by Dwjuan F. Fox, Jennifer L. Fox, and Jonnie M. Fox. Together, they have years of combined experience that will help them manage the company’s daily operations with a team of exceptional providers. They will hire and train additional employees to provide quality care for the residents. These employees will be passionate about caring for others and they will deliver outstanding client care. Creating the Madison House community is not only a fulfillment of their vision to create a loving environment for the elder population but is also an attractive business model.

Functioning as a non-profit, the facility will rely on funding through grants, charities and financial contributions to supplement Madison House operating cost. The company is seeking $700,000 in funding to develop the intended facility, furnish the spaces, hire employees, and implement operational processes. Once the company completes these projects it will be able to begin providing quality care to an elderly and often vulnerable population. However, with the successful opening of additional locations, planed for year four of operation, the business will start to reflect a profit for the organization with margins from 30% up to 50% from the previous years.

Expectations are to expand to three to four locations in the next ten years. This business plan contains additional detailed information about the business and its plans to provide service as a leading care company in Michigan. The plan includes sales, marketing, and growth expectations. Madison House expects steady growth until the facility reaches maximum occupancy to set the stage to open additional locations.

1.1 Company Summary

Madison House (MH) is a 501(c)3 organization based in Detroit, Michigan. The organization will open a boutique-style home that addresses the lack of home care services for aging adults who want to maintain independent living. Madison House provides a state of the art, environmentally conscious, neighborhood centered home with twenty-four-hour care for active seniors who many need assistance with meal plans, cooking, exercise, physical therapy, housekeeping, daily living assistance, and transportation.

Assisted living centers play a very important role in society. The industry is thriving as individuals seek opportunities to live with some level of independence, and care providers seek effective ways to improve patient care outcomes. The industry saves the healthcare system billions of dollars each year by providing a more cost-effective means of care when compared to full care centers. MH aims to provide professional, efficient, accurate, and caring services to all clients. The company will provide compassionate, dependable, and reliable care so clients can remain in a home-like environment where they feel safe and secure.

MH is a minority and veteran owned and operated family business. Owners, Dwjuan F. Fox, Jennifer L. Fox, and Jonnie M. Fox, will work to complete the facility renovation, install specialty and health/wellness elements, hire staff, and prepare to serve residents.
