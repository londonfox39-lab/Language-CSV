Care & Services

Personalized support in a safe, respectful, home-like setting.

Madison House provides active lifestyle assisted living with twenty-four-hour support and individualized care plans. Our services are designed for seniors and veterans who may need assistance with daily tasks while still wanting independence and dignity.

Core Services

  • Personal Care: Support with bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, and daily living activities.
  • Meals and Nutrition: Nutritious meals and hydration tailored to each resident's needs and preferences.
  • Housekeeping and Laundry: Regular cleaning, laundry assistance, and upkeep of living spaces.
  • Health and Wellness Programs: Exercise support, wellness education, and physical therapy coordination as needed.
  • Social and Recreational Activities: Community engagement, meaningful events, and welcoming shared spaces for residents and families.
  • 24/7 Safety and Support: Around-the-clock caregiver availability, supervision, and emergency response readiness.

Our Care Philosophy

We focus on compassionate, respectful, and personalized care for every resident. Our team builds strong relationships with residents and families, maintains clear communication, and supports both physical and emotional well-being.

Madison House is committed to continuous quality improvement, staff development, and a living environment where residents feel safe, valued, and connected.
